# Yahweh's Sword — Privacy Policy / 隐私政策（雅伟之剑）

Last updated / 最后更新：2026-09-24　Publisher / 发布者：Yahweh's People　Contact / 联系：support@yahwehword.com

## English

**Summary.** Yahweh's Sword has no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no analytics. Your notes, highlights, bookmarks, reading position and settings stay on your device.

**Data stored on your device.** Notes, highlights, bookmarks, search history and settings are saved locally. We do not receive them. You can export them to a file and import them on another device yourself.

**Crash reports.** If the app hits an unexpected error, it may send one report to our server (hosted on Netlify) that is forwarded by email (via Resend) to support@yahwehword.com. It contains: the error message and stack trace, app version, operating system, language setting, a random per-launch session ID, the name of the screen you were on, the last few screen names you visited, and screen size/OS version/user agent. It does not contain your notes, highlights, searches or any text you typed. It is used only to fix bugs.

**Network requests.** The app downloads Bible and study data from our data host (yswords-data.netlify.app), and checks GitHub (api.github.com) for new versions. These servers see your IP address as any website does. We do not link it to you.

**Feedback.** If you use the feedback form, what you type, including any contact details you choose to give, is emailed to us to reply and improve the app.

**Advertising, tracking, sale of data.** None. We do not sell personal data and do not track you across apps or sites.

**Children.** The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

**Bible texts.** Copyright and licence notices for each text are in the app's About page.

**Changes.** If this changes, we will update this page and its date.

## 中文

**概要。** 雅伟之剑没有账号、广告、应用内购买，也不做使用分析。你的笔记、标注、书签、阅读位置和设置都只保存在你的设备上。

**本机数据。** 笔记、标注、书签、搜索记录和设置保存在本机，我们收不到。你可以自行导出为文件，再在另一台设备导入。

**崩溃报告。** 应用遇到意外错误时，可能向我们的服务器（托管于 Netlify）发送一份报告，并经 Resend 转发到 support@yahwehword.com。内容包括：错误信息与堆栈、应用版本、操作系统、语言设置、每次启动随机生成的会话编号、出错时所在页面名称、最近几个访问过的页面名称，以及屏幕尺寸、系统版本和 User Agent。不包含你的笔记、标注、搜索内容或任何你输入的文字。仅用于修复问题。

**网络请求。** 应用会从我们的数据服务器（yswords-data.netlify.app）下载圣经与研经数据，并访问 GitHub（api.github.com）检查新版本。这些服务器会像任何网站一样看到你的 IP 地址，我们不会将其与你个人关联。

**反馈。** 如果你使用反馈表单，你填写的内容（包括你自愿提供的联系方式）会以邮件发给我们，用于回复和改进应用。

**广告、追踪与出售数据。** 一律没有。我们不出售个人数据，也不跨应用或网站追踪你。

**儿童。** 应用不会有意收集任何人的个人信息。

**圣经译本。** 各译本的版权与授权说明见应用「关于」页面。

**更新。** 如有变化，我们会更新本页及日期。
