# Reading plans (back 2026-10-15)

Words only. Sword is a study workbench, not a daily-reading app, and has no plans.

## What it is
Six plans, picked on `/reading-plan` (home card "Reading plan", Settings → Home sections):
`bible-1y` (whole Bible in a year, Bible order), `bible-1y-ot-nt` (OT and NT together; NT ends day 260),
`mcheyne` (M'Cheyne 1842, four readings a day), `nt-90`, `gospels-30`, `psalms-proverbs-31`.
A reader starts a plan on any date; day n is n-1 days after the start date. Days can be ticked in any order.

## Where things are
- Data: `assets/reading_plans.json`, GENERATED by `tools/build_reading_plans.py` (never edit by hand;
  `--check` is a test). Derived plans are cut into whole chapters balanced by verse count from
  `assets/kjv.json`; each chapter once, in order. No "chronological" plan: nothing in the repo to verify one against.
- M'Cheyne: `tools/data/mcheyne_1842.json` (public domain). Extracted from Ben Edgington's transcription
  of the 1842 calendar (edginet.org PDF, via pdftotext -bbox), checked day by day against Will Norris's WordPress
  plugin (cchmb/mcheyne-reading-plan) and jpillion/daily-readings: all 365 days agree, including the 37
  verse windows (Psalm 119 in seven parts, Exodus 11:1-12:21 …). One other copy (scottdc92/christian_json)
  disagrees from August 4 on and was NOT used. The calendar has no February 29.
- Code: `lib/services/reading_plan_service.dart` (plans, progress, merge, status), `lib/pages/reading_plan_page.dart`,
  home card `_ReadingPlanCard` in `dashboard_page.dart`. Strings: `readingPlan_*` in `ui_strings.dart`.
- Tests: `test/reading_plans_test.dart` (26).

## Rules that came from the first version's failure (removed v1.2.69, hotfix v1.3.45)
- Progress is ONE key with no dot, `readingPlan`, a JSON blob, profile-scoped. Dotted `plan.*` keys are rejected
  by Firebase RTDB and once broke every sync write. A test forbids `'plan.` in the sync layers.
- Sync merges by `mergeReadingPlanBlobs`: same plan + same start → union of days read; otherwise the newer
  choice wins; a stopped plan is a timestamped blob so a stop is not undone by a stale plan on another device.
- Day arithmetic uses UTC dates (a daylight-saving change must not skip or repeat a day).
- The old `plan.*` prefs of long-time readers are left alone; nothing reads or deletes them.
- Plan data is in the web offline pack list (`offline_pack_service.dart`).

## Not done

## The reader opened from a plan (2026-10-16)

Tapping a passage chip opens the reader as a `ReadingSession`
(`reading_plan_service.dart`): the day's chapters, in the plan's order,
and nothing else. Genesis 1-3 is three pages.

- the pager (`_paneChapters` in `bible_reading_pane.dart`) pages only
  those chapters; swipe, the bottom bar's arrows and the keyboard stop
  at the ends, and the book chip no longer opens the book picker;
- the top-left button is a back arrow to the plan (not Home), and the
  header's search is hidden;
- a strip over the bottom bar shows "Chapter n of N" with a progress
  bar; on the last chapter it offers "Mark today as read", which sets
  the day done and returns to the plan;
- a day with several passages (M'Cheyne) is one session across all of
  them. A passage that starts inside a chapter still reads as whole
  chapters; the reader opens on its first verse;
- if the reader ends up on a chapter outside the day (a jump from
  elsewhere) the session quietly steps aside and the ordinary reader
  pager takes over.

The Home page's Quick links also carry a "Reading plans" tile.
