Having trouble joining?

“App not available” can mean the testing release is not yet active or your account is not eligible. Use the same personal Google account for the group, opt-in page and Play Store. A school or work account may restrict Google Groups.

If the group page is unavailable, email support with the Google account you want to test with. Group membership alone does not opt you into either app.

Google Play testers: stay opted in and use the app regularly for at least 14 days. Each app has its own opt-in.

Prefer a standalone installer? GitHub lists the latest released packages and their version. Store editions update through their own store.

Tell us the app name, device, version and what happened. Never send passwords or verification codes.
