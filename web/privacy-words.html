English

Summary. Yahweh's Words has no advertising, no in-app purchases and no tracking. You can use it without an account, in which case your data stays on your device. If you choose to sign in, your reading data is synced to our cloud so it follows you across devices.

Without an account. Notes, highlights, bookmarks, reading plan progress, search history and settings are stored on your device only. Profiles on the device are a local way to keep people's data apart; they are not accounts.

If you sign in (optional). You can sign in with Google or with an email and password, through Google Firebase Authentication. We receive your sign-in identifier (email address and, for Google, your name and profile photo) and store it with Firebase. When signed in, the following are synced to Firebase (Google Cloud), under an ID tied to your account: highlights, notes and note titles, bookmarks, search history, reading position and app preferences. This data is used only to show it back to you on your other devices. Google processes it under its own terms and privacy policy.

Deleting your data. To delete your account and synced data, email support@yahwehword.com from the address you signed in with, and we will delete it. You can also stop syncing by signing out; data on your device stays until you clear it.

AI features. If you use AI search or AI word explanations, the text you enter (such as a search phrase or a word) is sent to our server and then to Google's Gemini service to produce the answer. Do not enter personal information into these boxes.

Crash reports. If the app hits an unexpected error, it may send one report to our server, forwarded by email (via Resend) to support@yahwehword.com. It contains: the error message and stack trace, app version, operating system, language, a random per-launch session ID, screen names, a short trail of recent navigation and playback events (such as which sermon audio ID was played), and screen size/OS version/user agent. It does not contain your notes, highlights or searches.

Embedded media. Some pages play YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com) or SoundCloud content. Those providers may set cookies or collect data under their own policies when you play it.

Network requests. The app downloads Bible and study data from yswords-data.netlify.app, checks GitHub (api.github.com) for new versions in the versions distributed outside the Microsoft Store, and loads fonts from Google Fonts on some platforms. These servers see your IP address as any website does.

Feedback. If you use the feedback form, what you type is emailed to us.

Advertising, tracking, sale of data. None. We do not sell personal data.

Children. Everyone, including children, can read the Bible in the app without an account, and in that case none of your reading data leaves your device. Signing in is optional and is meant for people old enough to hold a Google or email account (13+, or with a parent's permission). We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child through sign-in; if you believe a child has signed up, email us and we will delete the account.

Bible texts. Copyright and licence notices are in the app's About page.

Changes. If this changes, we will update this page and its date.

中文

概要。 雅伟的话没有广告、应用内购买或追踪。你可以不注册账号使用，此时数据只保存在本机。如果你选择登录，你的阅读数据会同步到我们的云端，以便在多台设备间使用。

不登录时。 笔记、标注、书签、读经计划进度、搜索记录和设置只保存在本机。设备上的「个人档案」只是在本机区分不同使用者的方式，不是账号。

登录后（可选）。 你可以用 Google 或邮箱加密码登录，由 Google Firebase Authentication 处理。我们会收到你的登录标识（邮箱；Google 登录还包括姓名和头像），并保存在 Firebase。登录后，以下内容会同步到 Firebase（Google Cloud），并与你的账号 ID 关联：标注、笔记及标题、书签、搜索记录、阅读位置和应用偏好。这些数据只用于在你的其他设备上还原，Google 按其自身条款和隐私政策处理。

删除数据。 如需删除账号及已同步的数据，请用登录邮箱发信至 support@yahwehword.com，我们会删除。你也可以退出登录以停止同步；本机上的数据会保留，直到你自己清除。

AI 功能。 使用 AI 搜索或 AI 释义时，你输入的文字（如搜索词或词语）会发送到我们的服务器，再转给 Google Gemini 生成结果。请勿在这些输入框中填写个人信息。

崩溃报告。 应用遇到意外错误时，可能向我们的服务器发送一份报告，并经 Resend 转发到 support@yahwehword.com。内容包括：错误信息与堆栈、应用版本、操作系统、语言、每次启动随机生成的会话编号、页面名称、最近的导航与播放记录（如播放了哪一篇讲道的编号），以及屏幕尺寸、系统版本和 User Agent。不包含你的笔记、标注或搜索内容。

内嵌媒体。 部分页面会播放 YouTube（youtube-nocookie.com）或 SoundCloud 的内容，这些服务商在你播放时可能按其自身政策设置 Cookie 或收集数据。

网络请求。 应用从 yswords-data.netlify.app 下载圣经与研经数据；在 Microsoft Store 之外分发的版本还会访问 GitHub（api.github.com）检查新版本；部分平台会从 Google Fonts 加载字体。这些服务器会像任何网站一样看到你的 IP 地址。

反馈。 如果你使用反馈表单，你填写的内容会以邮件发给我们。

广告、追踪与出售数据。 一律没有。我们不出售个人数据。

儿童。 所有人（包括儿童）都可以不注册账号阅读圣经，此时你的阅读数据不会离开本机。登录是可选的，适合已达到 Google 或邮箱账号年龄要求的人（13 岁以上，或经家长同意）。我们不会有意通过登录收集儿童的个人信息；如果你认为有儿童注册了账号，请来信告知，我们会删除。

圣经译本。 版权与授权说明见应用「关于」页面。

更新。 如有变化，我们会更新本页及日期。
