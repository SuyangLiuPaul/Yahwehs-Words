English

Summary. Yahweh's Words has no advertising, no in-app purchases and no tracking. You can use it without an account, in which case your personal reading data stays on your device. If you choose to sign in, your reading data is synced to our cloud so it follows you across devices.

Without an account. Notes, highlights, bookmarks, reading plan progress, search history and settings are stored on your device only. Profiles on the device are a local way to keep people's data apart; they are not accounts.

If you sign in (optional). You can sign in with Google, Sign in with Apple on iOS and macOS, or with an email and password, through Google Firebase Authentication. We receive your sign-in identifier (email address, including a private relay address if you use one, and, where provided, your name and profile photo) and store it with Firebase. When signed in, the following are synced to Firebase (Google Cloud), under an ID tied to your account: highlights, notes and note titles, bookmarks, search history, reading position and app preferences. This data is used only to show it back to you on your other devices. Google processes it under its own terms and privacy policy.

Deleting your data. On iOS or macOS, go to Settings → Account → Delete account to remove your account and synced data. You can also email support@yahwehword.com from the address you signed in with and we will delete it. Signing out stops syncing; data on your device stays until you clear it.

AI features. If you use AI search or AI word explanations, the text you enter (such as a search phrase or a word) is sent to our server and then to Google's Gemini service to produce the answer. Do not enter personal information into these boxes.

Crash reports. If the app hits an unexpected error, it may send one report to our server, forwarded by email (via Resend) to support@yahwehword.com. It contains: the error message and stack trace, app version, operating system, language, a random per-launch session ID, screen names, a short trail of recent navigation and playback events (such as which sermon audio ID was played), and screen size/OS version/user agent. It does not contain your notes, highlights or searches.

Paired watch and car audio. Optional companions exchange the daily Bible verse, available audio titles, playback position and basic playback commands with your paired phone. The watch caches the daily verse locally for offline reading. These features do not share your account credentials, notes or bookmarks with the companion. Audio plays on the phone; the app does not record your microphone.

Embedded media. Some pages play YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com) or SoundCloud content. Those providers may set cookies or collect data under their own policies when you play it.

Anonymous usage totals. App launches and top-level page views increment daily totals in our administration database (Firebase Realtime Database). The counters contain the UTC day, app name and broad page category. They do not contain account or device identifiers, full passage or media paths, notes, search text, or individual visit times. They are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Network providers still receive the IP address needed to serve the request.

Network requests. The app downloads Bible and study data from yswords-data.netlify.app, reads public announcements and channel-specific release information from our administration database, checks GitHub (api.github.com) in direct-download builds and uses the relevant store update mechanisms in store builds, and loads fonts from Google Fonts on some platforms. These servers see your IP address as any website does.

Feedback. Submitting feedback is optional. Your message, category and any name or reply address you provide are emailed through Netlify and Resend to our support team and copied to the administration inbox in Firebase Realtime Database. The request includes app/version, language and available technical context such as screen, operating system, browser, time zone and Bible reading position. The support email can also include the request IP address and approximate country/region supplied by the hosting provider; it does not request GPS location. In Words, a signed-in email may be included to help reply. These details are used to respond and diagnose the reported issue.

Advertising, tracking, sale of data. None. We do not sell personal data.

Children. Everyone, including children, can read the Bible in the app without an account, and in that case none of your reading data leaves your device. Signing in is optional and is meant for people old enough to hold a Google or email account (13+, or with a parent's permission). We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child through sign-in; if you believe a child has signed up, email us and we will delete the account.

Bible texts. Copyright and licence notices are in the app's About page.

Changes. If this changes, we will update this page and its date.

中文

概要。 雅伟的话没有广告、应用内购买或追踪。你可以不注册账号使用，此时个人阅读数据只保存在本机。如果你选择登录，你的阅读数据会同步到我们的云端，以便在多台设备间使用。

不登录时。 笔记、标注、书签、读经计划进度、搜索记录和设置只保存在本机。设备上的「个人档案」只是在本机区分不同使用者的方式，不是账号。

登录后（可选）。 你可以用 Google、iOS 与 macOS 上的 Apple 登录，或邮箱加密码登录，由 Google Firebase Authentication 处理。我们会收到你的登录标识（邮箱，包括你选择使用的私密转发地址；如有提供，还包括姓名和头像），并保存在 Firebase。登录后，以下内容会同步到 Firebase（Google Cloud），并与你的账号 ID 关联：标注、笔记及标题、书签、搜索记录、阅读位置和应用偏好。这些数据只用于在你的其他设备上还原，Google 按其自身条款和隐私政策处理。

删除数据。 在 iOS 或 macOS 上，进入「设置 → 账号 → 删除账号」可删除账号及已同步的数据。你也可以用登录邮箱发信至 support@yahwehword.com，我们会删除。退出登录会停止同步；本机上的数据会保留，直到你自己清除。

AI 功能。 使用 AI 搜索或 AI 释义时，你输入的文字（如搜索词或词语）会发送到我们的服务器，再转给 Google Gemini 生成结果。请勿在这些输入框中填写个人信息。

崩溃报告。 应用遇到意外错误时，可能向我们的服务器发送一份报告，并经 Resend 转发到 support@yahwehword.com。内容包括：错误信息与堆栈、应用版本、操作系统、语言、每次启动随机生成的会话编号、页面名称、最近的导航与播放记录（如播放了哪一篇讲道的编号），以及屏幕尺寸、系统版本和 User Agent。不包含你的笔记、标注或搜索内容。

配对手表与车载音频。 可选的配套功能会与配对手机交换每日经文、可用音频标题、播放位置及基本播放指令。手表会在本机缓存每日经文，以便离线阅读。这些功能不会向配套设备发送账号凭据、笔记或书签。音频由手机播放，应用不会录制麦克风。

内嵌媒体。 部分页面会播放 YouTube（youtube-nocookie.com）或 SoundCloud 的内容，这些服务商在你播放时可能按其自身政策设置 Cookie 或收集数据。

匿名使用计数。 应用启动及主要页面访问会累加到管理后台数据库（Firebase Realtime Database）的每日总数。计数只包含 UTC 日期、应用名称和主要页面类别，不包含账号或设备标识、完整经文或媒体路径、笔记、搜索词或个人访问时间，不用于广告或跨应用追踪。处理网络请求的服务商仍会收到连接所需的 IP 地址。

网络请求。 应用从 yswords-data.netlify.app 下载圣经与研经数据；从管理后台数据库读取公开公告与各渠道版本信息；直接下载版本可访问 GitHub（api.github.com）检查新版本，商店版本使用所属商店的更新机制；部分平台会从 Google Fonts 加载字体。这些服务器会像任何网站一样看到你的 IP 地址。

反馈。 提交反馈是可选的。你的消息、类别及自愿填写的姓名或回复邮箱会经 Netlify 和 Resend 发给支持人员，并复制到 Firebase Realtime Database 的管理后台收件箱。请求会附带应用与版本、语言，以及可用的页面、系统、浏览器、时区和读经位置等技术信息。支持邮件还可能包含托管服务提供的请求 IP 地址和大致国家或地区，不会请求 GPS 位置。Words 登录后的邮箱可能随反馈发送，以便回复。这些信息用于答复及排查你报告的问题。

广告、追踪与出售数据。 一律没有。我们不出售个人数据。

儿童。 所有人（包括儿童）都可以不注册账号阅读圣经，此时你的阅读数据不会离开本机。登录是可选的，适合已达到 Google 或邮箱账号年龄要求的人（13 岁以上，或经家长同意）。我们不会有意通过登录收集儿童的个人信息；如果你认为有儿童注册了账号，请来信告知，我们会删除。

圣经译本。 版权与授权说明见应用「关于」页面。

更新。 如有变化，我们会更新本页及日期。
