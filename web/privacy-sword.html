English

Summary. Yahweh's Sword has no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases. Anonymous daily usage totals help us improve the app. Your notes, highlights, bookmarks, reading position and settings stay on your device.

Data stored on your device. Notes, highlights, bookmarks, search history and settings are saved locally. We do not receive them. You can export them to a file and import them on another device yourself.

Crash reports. If the app hits an unexpected error, it may send one report to our server (hosted on Netlify) that is forwarded by email (via Resend) to support@yahwehword.com. It contains: the error message and stack trace, app version, operating system, language setting, a random per-launch session ID, the name of the screen you were on, the last few screen names you visited, and screen size/OS version/user agent. It does not contain your notes, highlights, searches or any text you typed. It is used only to fix bugs.

Anonymous usage totals. App launches and top-level page views increment daily totals in our administration database (Firebase Realtime Database). The counters contain the UTC day, app name and broad page category. They do not contain account or device identifiers, full passage or media paths, notes, search text, or individual visit times. They are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Network providers still receive the IP address needed to serve the request.

Network requests. The app downloads Bible and study data from our data host (yswords-data.netlify.app), and reads public announcements and channel-specific release information from our administration database. Store builds use their store update mechanisms; direct downloads can check GitHub (api.github.com) for new versions. These servers see your IP address as any website does. We do not link it to you.

Feedback. Submitting feedback is optional. Your message, category and any name or reply address you provide are emailed through Netlify and Resend to our support team and copied to the administration inbox in Firebase Realtime Database. The request includes app/version, language and available technical context such as screen, operating system, browser, time zone and Bible reading position. The support email can also include the request IP address and approximate country/region supplied by the hosting provider; it does not request GPS location. These details are used to respond and diagnose the reported issue.

Advertising, tracking, sale of data. None. We do not sell personal data and do not track you across apps or sites.

Children. Bible reading does not require an account. We do not knowingly request children’s personal information; please avoid including it in optional support messages.

Bible texts. Copyright and licence notices for each text are in the app's About page.

Changes. If this changes, we will update this page and its date.

中文

概要。 雅伟之剑没有账号、广告、应用内购买，会汇总匿名的每日使用计数，以帮助改进应用。你的笔记、标注、书签、阅读位置和设置都只保存在你的设备上。

本机数据。 笔记、标注、书签、搜索记录和设置保存在本机，我们收不到。你可以自行导出为文件，再在另一台设备导入。

崩溃报告。 应用遇到意外错误时，可能向我们的服务器（托管于 Netlify）发送一份报告，并经 Resend 转发到 support@yahwehword.com。内容包括：错误信息与堆栈、应用版本、操作系统、语言设置、每次启动随机生成的会话编号、出错时所在页面名称、最近几个访问过的页面名称，以及屏幕尺寸、系统版本和 User Agent。不包含你的笔记、标注、搜索内容或任何你输入的文字。仅用于修复问题。

匿名使用计数。 应用启动及主要页面访问会累加到管理后台数据库（Firebase Realtime Database）的每日总数。计数只包含 UTC 日期、应用名称和主要页面类别，不包含账号或设备标识、完整经文或媒体路径、笔记、搜索词或个人访问时间，不用于广告或跨应用追踪。处理网络请求的服务商仍会收到连接所需的 IP 地址。

网络请求。 应用会从我们的数据服务器（yswords-data.netlify.app）下载圣经与研经数据，并从管理后台数据库读取公开公告与各渠道版本信息。商店版本使用所属商店的更新机制；直接下载版本可访问 GitHub（api.github.com）检查新版本。这些服务器会像任何网站一样看到你的 IP 地址，我们不会将其与你个人关联。

反馈。 提交反馈是可选的。你的消息、类别及自愿填写的姓名或回复邮箱会经 Netlify 和 Resend 发给支持人员，并复制到 Firebase Realtime Database 的管理后台收件箱。请求会附带应用与版本、语言，以及可用的页面、系统、浏览器、时区和读经位置等技术信息。支持邮件还可能包含托管服务提供的请求 IP 地址和大致国家或地区，不会请求 GPS 位置。这些信息用于答复及排查你报告的问题。

广告、追踪与出售数据。 一律没有。我们不出售个人数据，也不跨应用或网站追踪你。

儿童。 阅读圣经无需账号。我们不会有意请求儿童的个人信息；请勿在可选的支持消息中填写这些信息。

圣经译本。 各译本的版权与授权说明见应用「关于」页面。

更新。 如有变化，我们会更新本页及日期。
